01 // The Manifesto

Moving Together.
From the Bridge to the Atlantic.

Lagos is a city of relentless kinetic ambition. But at 05:30 in the morning, before the horns blare and the yellow danfos flood the expressways, the coastal roads belong to us.

The Runners is an independent, non-commercial collective born on the Lekki-Ikoyi Link Bridge. We do not sell apparel, running shoes, or supplements. We have no paywalled memberships. We gather every Saturday and Tuesday morning for the pure, unadulterated discipline of morning movement, deep conversational pacing, and genuine Lagos community.

04 // Pace Crews

Find Your Cadence.
No Runner Left Behind. Ever.

Every Saturday run is organized into four distinct pace packs led by experienced community pacemakers. Whether you are chasing a 20-minute 5K or lacing up for the first time, there is a crew matched to your breath.

01

The Cheetahs

4:30 – 5:15 min/km

High tempo, laser-focused breathing, fast turnover. Built for seasoned athletes, track regulars, and marathoners sharpening their race-day sharpness.

02

The Cadence

5:30 – 6:15 min/km

Our largest endurance pack. Rhythmic, steady strides, balanced aerobic effort, and perfect for intermediate runners expanding their distance.

03

The Cruisers

6:30 – 7:15 min/km

The conversation pack. Smooth aerobic pace where you can comfortably chat about life, tech, and weekend plans while chewing through the kilometers.

04

Party Pace & Beginners

7:30 – 8:30+ min/km

Walk/Run intervals, zero ego, pure joy. If this is your first time running in Lagos, our dedicated sweeper captains will guide you every meter of the way.

05 // The Rituals

What Happens at the Finish Line.
The Lagos Saturday Morning Protocol.

🥥

Fresh Admiralty Coconuts

Live chopped young green coconuts at the Admiralty Way finish line. Cold natural electrolytes and sweet coconut water directly from the husk.

🌺

Ice-Cold Zobo Spritz

Craft brewed Nigerian hibiscus, crushed ginger, and cloves chilled over ice. Antioxidant recovery punch designed specifically for morning hydration.

☕

Lagos Cold Brew Banter

Freshly ground specialty coffee from local artisan roasters. Stick around for breakfast banter, work talk, and genuine cross-industry connections.

🎶

170 BPM Sound System

Afrobeats, Amapiano, and high-cadence electronic grooves playing from our portable sound rigs to keep the positive frequency vibrating long after the run.

07 // Information

First-Timer Guide.
Everything You Need to Know.

Yes, 100% free. There are no registration fees, monthly dues, or hidden costs. We are an open athletic collective supported by community volunteers who love the city of Lagos.
No. We explicitly do not sell running gear, shoes, apparel, or equipment. Our entire mission is strictly focused on community, route planning, safety, and bringing runners together across Lagos. Wear whatever running clothes you already own!
We have designated, well-lit parking with local security guards stationed directly adjacent to the Admiralty Way Toll Plaza starting from 05:15 AM every Saturday. You can leave your vehicle with total peace of mind.
Our Pace Crew 4 (The Party Pace) was created specifically for you! We run at a friendly conversational pace with walk breaks whenever needed. There is always a sweeper runner at the very back to ensure no one is ever left behind.
A comfortable pair of running shoes, a water bottle, a small running belt or pocket for your car keys, and high energy. Fresh coconut water and electrolyte spritzers are provided at the finish line!
